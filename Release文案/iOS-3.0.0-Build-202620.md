## 灵动照片 iOS 3.0.0（Build 202620）

本 Release 为“灵动照片”iOS 3.0.0（Build 202620）的正式版本说明与对应公开源代码功能说明。

### 本次更新

- 新增原生 iPad 体验。iPad 现在使用独立侧栏组织创作模式与工具，支持横竖屏切换及可调整窗口；导入照片、切换功能、编辑和导出均针对大屏重新布局。
- 新增“灵动色卡”与“灵动月历”。色卡可将照片主色条向下、向左或向右延伸，月历会保留原图比例，并可记录拍摄日期、节气和位置信息。
- 新增“流光折扇”，可用六张照片组成完整展开的扇面；既可在本机自动寻找同一色系照片，也可自由换图、拖动和缩放，最终导出 2400 × 1800 透明 PNG。
- 新增“邮票制作”“明信片”和“邮集”。可使用多种比例与异形齿孔模板制作邮票，将照片、寄语、邮票和邮戳组合成明信片，并用带定位的作品点亮城市足迹。
- 邮集新增简体中文省份分组、城市解锁、封面选择和自由旋转展示；城市判断结合照片元数据与行政名称目录，定位不足时不会猜测城市。
- 新增“票根集”，可把旅行、演出或某段时光的影像票根整理成独立收藏。
- 新增“票根显影”，使用设备本机识别导入的实体票照片，并重制为可编辑、可验证的影像票根。
- 新增“琉璃档案”，以档案视图保存设备、镜头、拍摄参数、日期与坐标等原始记录。
- 扫描票根后新增“寻找本机原图”，可在获得照片访问授权后，于本机照片库中寻找当初生成票根的原片。
- 新增“浮光菜单”与“灵屿展开”两种模式入口，可在设置中选择；支持灵动岛的机型可从灵动岛下方展开功能入口。
- 新增空白主页主题、重新设计的首次使用引导，以及功能内快捷设定入口。
- 新增一次性“支持我们”内容，可解锁二十四节气诗意色名、典藏票面、更多邮票与明信片模板、四款专属应用图标及支持者标志；没有订阅和广告。
- 更新购买完成后的支持状态提示，统一显示“所有PRO功能都已解锁”。
- 完善简体中文、繁体中文与英文文案，优化 iPhone、iPad、App Clip、照片操作扩展和 Live Activity 的布局与运行稳定性，并修复若干已知问题。
- 版本更新为 3.0.0（Build 202620）。

### 主要功能

- 灵动卡片：根据照片内容与色彩生成文案和卡片排版，支持取色渐变与局部取色
- 灵动色卡：提取照片主色，并向下、向左或向右延伸色条而不遮挡原图
- 灵动月历：保留照片比例，组合拍摄日期、节气与可选位置信息
- 琉璃色盘：提取六种代表色，显示 HEX 色值、文学颜色名称及整图占比
- 光影解析：分析照片的明暗分布、高光与暗部细节，并提供直观说明
- 流光折扇：自动寻色或自由编排六张照片，生成完整透明扇面作品
- 邮票、明信片与邮集：制作和收藏邮票、组合明信片，并按城市整理带定位的足迹
- 票根收藏与显影：整理影像票根，在本机识别实体票，并重制为可验证作品
- 琉璃档案：保存照片的设备、镜头、参数、日期与坐标记录
- 视频转实况：从视频中选择连续三秒片段与自定义封面，在本机生成 Live Photo
- 一键手帐：支持最多五张照片组合、自动排版、文案与 Emoji
- 气泡印章：结合照片、文案、主色与真实 EXIF 信息生成摄影印章
- 色谱壁纸：根据照片主色生成适配设备尺寸的渐变色谱壁纸
- 隐私马赛克：支持手动涂抹、擦除，以及本机智能识别人脸、车牌、二维码和敏感文字
- 影像素描：在本机生成铅笔、炭笔和水彩风格作品
- 影像票根：使用照片、文字、公开信息与设备封印生成可分享、可扫描验证的票根
- 影像相簿：将普通照片和灵动作品编排成多页相簿，并导出电子版或印刷 PDF
- 色彩关系：比较两张照片的色彩共鸣、对照与过渡关系，并导出分析作品
- 扫描验证票根：使用相机扫描二维码，在设备本地读取并验证公开信息
- 寻找本机原图：根据票根信息，在获得授权的本机照片库中寻找对应原片

### iPad 与模式入口

- iPad 使用原生侧栏组织主要创作模式和独立工具，支持横竖屏、可调整窗口和大屏编辑布局
- iPhone 可选择“浮光菜单”或“灵屿展开”；不支持灵动岛的设备会自动使用兼容入口
- 未选择照片时可直接进入色彩关系、影像相簿、票根扫描、视频转实况、流光折扇、邮票、明信片与邮集等独立工具
- 切换创作模式时会保留当前照片和本机分析结果，无需重复选择图片

### 邮票、明信片与邮集

- 邮票支持多种比例、三角形、扇形与圆形模板，可调整构图、标题、日期、字体、字号和文字位置
- 明信片提供多种版式，可编辑寄语、日期、地址、字体与邮戳，并复用已经收藏的邮票
- 邮集只使用用户作品中已有的定位信息，不内置账号或云端足迹；无定位或定位精度不足的照片仍可制作普通邮票，但不会被错误归入城市
- 支持简体中文省份分组，以及英文、繁体中文环境下的城市网格；收藏、封面与排序均保存在应用本机

### 票根收藏、显影与档案

- 票根集支持按旅行、演出或时间主题分组，并在本机保存封面与票根索引
- 票根显影使用本机文字与画面识别读取实体票，并允许用户检查、修改识别结果后生成票根
- 标准票面可直接使用；典藏票面属于一次性支持内容
- 琉璃档案保存照片的原始拍摄记录，不会改写照片文件
- “寻找本机原图”只在用户主动操作并授予照片访问权限后执行，不会上传照片库内容

### 本地与隐私

照片识别、色彩提取、光影解析、色彩关系分析、实体票识别、素描处理、视频转实况、文案生成、二维码解析、设备签名与作品渲染均在设备本地完成。本项目不需要账户，不包含广告或订阅，也不会收集照片、创作内容及设备标识。

iOS 提供可选的一次性“支持我们”购买。购买与恢复通过 Apple StoreKit 完成；完整商业色彩资料及支持者内容不属于公开源代码发布范围。显示城市名称时可能使用 Apple 系统提供的地理编码服务。

> 能在设备上完成的事情，就应该留在设备上。

## Lingdong Photo iOS 3.0.0 (Build 202620)

This release contains the official release notes and public source-code feature overview for Lingdong Photo iOS 3.0.0 (Build 202620).

### What's New

- Added a native iPad experience with a dedicated sidebar, portrait and landscape layouts, and support for resizable windows. Importing, mode switching, editing, and exporting have all been adapted for larger screens.
- Added Motion Palette and Motion Calendar. Motion Palette extends a photo's dominant-color strip below, left, or right without covering the image. Motion Calendar preserves the original aspect ratio and can display the capture date, solar term, and optional location.
- Added Flowing-Light Fan. Arrange six photos into a fully opened fan using on-device color matching or manual replacement, positioning, and scaling, then export a 2400 × 1800 transparent PNG.
- Added Stamp Maker, Postcard, and Stamp Collection. Create stamps with multiple proportional and shaped perforation templates, combine photos, messages, stamps, and postmarks into postcards, and use geotagged creations to reveal visited cities.
- Added Simplified Chinese province grouping, city unlocking, cover selection, and free-rotation presentation to Stamp Collection. City resolution combines photo metadata with an administrative-name catalog and does not guess when location precision is insufficient.
- Added Photo Ticket Collections for organizing tickets from trips, performances, and meaningful periods.
- Added Photo Ticket Developing, which recognizes an imported physical ticket on the device and recreates it as an editable, verifiable Photo Ticket.
- Added Glass Archive for preserving original device, lens, capture-setting, date, and coordinate records.
- Added Find Original on This Device after scanning a Photo Ticket. With Photos permission, the app can search the local library for the original image used to create the ticket.
- Added Floating Menu and Island Reveal as two selectable mode-entry styles. Supported Dynamic Island devices can expand the entry panel below the island.
- Added empty-screen themes, a redesigned onboarding experience, and direct settings entry points inside supported tools.
- Added optional one-time Support Us content, including poetic 24-solar-term color names, collector ticket designs, additional stamp and postcard templates, four alternate app icons, and a supporter badge. There are no subscriptions or advertisements.
- Updated the post-purchase Support Us status to clearly state that all PRO features are unlocked.
- Refined Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, and English copy, improved iPhone, iPad, App Clip, Photos action extension, and Live Activity layouts and stability, and fixed several known issues.
- Updated the version to 3.0.0 (Build 202620).

Requires iOS 17.0 or later and supports iPhone and iPad. Photo recognition, color and light analysis, Photo Ticket parsing, physical-ticket recognition, and artwork rendering are performed on the device.

### Main Features

- Motion Card: Generates copy and card layouts from photo content and colors, with sampled-color gradients and local color sampling
- Motion Palette: Extracts dominant colors and extends the color strip below, left, or right without covering the photo
- Motion Calendar: Preserves the photo's aspect ratio and combines it with capture date, solar term, and optional location
- Glass Palette: Extracts six representative colors and displays HEX values, literary color names, and whole-image percentages
- Light Analysis: Analyzes brightness distribution, highlight and shadow detail, and provides approachable explanations
- Flowing-Light Fan: Uses color matching or manual arrangement to combine six photos into a transparent fan artwork
- Stamp, Postcard, and Stamp Collection: Creates and collects stamps and postcards, with local organization by geotagged city
- Photo Ticket Collections and Developing: Organizes Photo Tickets, recognizes physical tickets on the device, and recreates them as verifiable artwork
- Glass Archive: Preserves device, lens, capture-setting, date, and coordinate records
- Video to Live Photo: Selects a continuous three-second video segment and custom key photo to create a Live Photo on the device
- Instant Journal: Combines up to five photos with automatic layouts, generated copy, and Emoji
- Bubble Stamp: Creates a photographic stamp from the photo, copy, primary color, and real EXIF information
- Spectrum Wallpaper: Generates a device-sized gradient spectrum wallpaper from the photo's primary colors
- Privacy Mosaic: Supports manual painting and erasing, plus on-device detection of faces, license plates, QR codes, and sensitive text
- Photo Sketch: Creates pencil, charcoal, and watercolor-style artwork on the device
- Photo Ticket: Creates shareable and scannable tickets from photos, text, selected public information, and a device seal
- Photo Album: Arranges regular photos and Lingdong artworks into multi-page albums for digital or print PDF export
- Color Relationship: Compares color resonance, contrast, and transition between two photos and exports an analysis artwork
- Scan Photo Ticket: Scans a QR code with the camera and reads and verifies its public information locally
- Find Original on This Device: Uses Photo Ticket information to locate the corresponding original in the authorized local photo library

### iPad and Mode Entry

- iPad uses a native sidebar for creation modes and independent tools, with rotation, resizable-window support, and large-screen editing layouts.
- iPhone users can choose Floating Menu or Island Reveal. Devices without Dynamic Island automatically use a compatible presentation.
- Independent tools such as Color Relationship, Photo Album, Scan Photo Ticket, Video to Live Photo, Flowing-Light Fan, Stamp Maker, Postcard, and Stamp Collection can be opened before selecting a photo.
- Switching creation modes preserves the current photo and its on-device analysis results.

### Stamps, Postcards, and Stamp Collection

- Stamp Maker supports multiple proportions, triangles, fan shapes, and circles, with composition, title, date, font, size, and text-position controls.
- Postcard offers multiple layouts with editable messages, dates, addresses, fonts, and postmarks, and can reuse collected stamps.
- Stamp Collection uses location information already present in user-created works. Photos without location or sufficient precision can still create regular stamps but are not assigned to a guessed city.
- Simplified Chinese uses province grouping, while English and Traditional Chinese retain a city-grid presentation. Collections, covers, and ordering remain on the device.

### Photo Ticket Collections, Developing, and Archive

- Photo Ticket Collections organize tickets by trip, performance, or time-based theme and keep covers and indexes locally.
- Photo Ticket Developing uses on-device text and image recognition for physical tickets and lets the user review and edit recognized information before creating a ticket.
- Standard ticket designs remain available; collector designs are part of the optional one-time Support Us content.
- Glass Archive preserves original capture records without modifying the photo file.
- Find Original on This Device runs only after an explicit user action and Photos authorization, and never uploads the photo library.

### Local Processing and Privacy

Photo recognition, color extraction, Light Analysis, Color Relationship analysis, physical-ticket recognition, sketch processing, Video to Live Photo conversion, copy generation, QR code parsing, device signing, and artwork rendering are performed locally on the device. The project requires no account, contains no advertising or subscriptions, and does not collect photos, creative content, or device identifiers.

The iOS app offers optional one-time Support Us content. Purchases and restoration are handled through Apple StoreKit. Complete commercial color data and supporter content are outside the public source release. Displaying city names may use Apple's system geocoding service.

> What can be completed on the device should stay on the device.
